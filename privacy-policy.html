Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CarPlayConnect ("the App") handles information when you use it. The App is operated by [YOUR FULL NAME], an individual developer.

Summary

CarPlayConnect does not collect, store, or share your personal data on any server we control. The App runs entirely on your device. It connects to third-party services (Spotify, Apple Maps, Radio Browser) only when you actively use those features, and only to deliver the functionality you request.

Information the App Uses

Location

The App uses your device's location to display your position on the map, provide navigation, and show nearby places (gas stations, parking, restaurants, etc.). Location data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to any server we control. You can revoke location access at any time in your device settings.

Contacts

If you use the phone/contact feature, the App accesses your contacts to display them for calling. Contact data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server we control.

Spotify

If you choose to connect your Spotify account, the App uses Spotify's official SDK and Web API to control playback and read your recently played tracks and saved songs. Your Spotify authentication tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain and are never transmitted to any server we control. Your use of Spotify is subject to Spotify's Privacy Policy.

Radio

The radio feature uses the Radio Browser public API to list internet radio stations. When you select a station, its stream is played directly from the broadcaster. We do not track which stations you listen to. Your favorite stations are saved only on your device.

Maps

Maps and place search are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). Map interactions are handled by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Storage

All settings, preferences, and favorites (such as your radio favorites, selected country, and unit preferences) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and Keychain). This data is not transmitted anywhere. If you uninstall the App, this data is removed.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party trackers. We do not build user profiles.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server we control, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. Data stored on your device is under your control at all times — you can clear it by uninstalling the App or by revoking permissions in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
