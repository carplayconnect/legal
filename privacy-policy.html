Plain-English Summary

✓ No account required. No name, email, or phone number needed to use the App.

✓ We do not sell your personal information.

✓ We do not use Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

1. Information the App May Access on Your Device

Certain features require access to information on your iPhone. In every case below, access occurs only when necessary for a feature you choose to use, and only after you grant the applicable iOS permission. This information stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers unless explicitly stated otherwise elsewhere in this Policy.

WhatWhyWhen
Location Show your position on the map, provide navigation and routing, find nearby places (gas stations, charging stations, parking, restaurants, pharmacies, and similar categories) via Apple Core Location and MapKit Only while you actively use the map or navigation features, and only after you grant Location permission
Contacts Display contacts within the driving interface via Apple's Contacts framework, so you can call a contact from the dashboard Only after you grant Contacts permission
Calling functionality Initiate calls from the driving interface using Apple-provided system functionality Only when you tap to call a contact; the App does not record calls or access call audio
App preferences Personalize your experience: distance units (km/mi), selected radio country, whether the screen stays on while driving, and your last-used music source Stored locally via iOS UserDefaults; updates whenever you change a setting

You can revoke Location or Contacts permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → [Permission]. We do not intentionally send your precise GPS coordinates, contact names, or phone numbers to Google Analytics for Firebase.

2. Music Playback

Spotify

If you choose to connect your Spotify account, the App uses the Spotify App Remote SDK and Spotify Web API to control playback (play, pause, skip) and to display your recently played tracks and saved songs. Your Spotify authentication tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain (encrypted, on-device) and are never transmitted to us. We do not intentionally send song titles, artist names, or your listening history to Google Analytics for Firebase. Your use of Spotify is also governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.

Apple Music

Apple Music integration is planned for a future update and is not yet available in the App. This Privacy Policy will be updated with the relevant details before that feature becomes active.

Radio

The App's radio feature uses the Radio Browser public, community-maintained API to list internet radio stations by country. When you select a station, the audio stream is delivered directly from the broadcaster — we do not operate our own streaming servers and do not track which stations you listen to. For your security, the App only lists stations that stream over HTTPS. Your favorite stations are stored only on your device.

3. Information Collected Through Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google LLC, to understand general App usage and to detect and fix crashes.

Analytics may process information such as: app opens and sessions, screens or features viewed, general feature interactions, session duration, app version, operating-system information, device model, language, approximate regional information, an automatically generated app-instance identifier, and Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) where applicable.

Crashlytics may process: stack traces, device model, iOS version, and app version at the time of a crash.

We do not intentionally send the following to Google Analytics for Firebase or Crashlytics: your name, email address, phone number, address book contents, precise GPS coordinates, Spotify listening history, or call contents.

This processing is anonymous and is not linked to your identity. Where applicable law (such as GDPR) requires a legal basis for this processing, we rely on legitimate interest — understanding aggregate usage and fixing crashes to keep the App reliable — rather than data that identifies you individually. The App does not currently offer an in-app control to disable Analytics; you may still limit data collection generally through your iOS device settings (e.g., disabling "Share iPhone Analytics" in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

4. Identifiers

App-Instance Identifier

Firebase generates an identifier associated with a particular installation of the App, used to calculate metrics such as active users and sessions. It is not created from your name, email, or phone number.

Identifier for Vendor (IDFV)

Firebase may use Apple's IDFV, an identifier shared across apps from the same developer on your device, for analytics purposes. We do not use IDFV to identify you by name or to track you across apps from unrelated developers.

Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)

The App does not use Apple's IDFA. We do not use IDFA for advertising, attribution, profiling, or cross-app tracking of any kind.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The App does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising networks, behavioral advertising, remarketing, or data brokers. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because the App does not engage in cross-app or cross-site tracking, it does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.

11. Data Retention

  • On-device data (favorites, preferences, Spotify tokens): stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Firebase Analytics events: retained by Google for up to 14 months, then automatically deleted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics reports: retained by Google for up to 90 days.

12. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS (radio stations using insecure HTTP streams are filtered out and not offered for playback). Spotify authentication tokens are stored in iOS Keychain (encrypted, hardware-backed). We do not operate our own servers, so no personal data is stored on infrastructure we control. iOS application sandboxing and permission controls provide additional protection. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we follow industry best practices.
